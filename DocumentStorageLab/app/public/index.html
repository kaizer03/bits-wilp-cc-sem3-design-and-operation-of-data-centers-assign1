          About this lab

          Express asks the SeaweedFS coordinator for storage locations, then transfers bytes to a storage service. MongoDB retains a shared catalogue entry for each uploaded file.

          Topic 7 — Infrastructure deployment

          Compose defines services, networks, resource limits and persistent storage. Recreating a service retains its mounted data.

          Topic 10 — Software defined storage

          Two copies are requested. Stop a storage service to compare read and write behaviour, then register a third service to inspect capacity.

          Receipts and file contents

          Every browser connected to this lab reads the same catalogue. Older receipt exports can be imported after their stored contents are verified. The export contains identifiers and hashes, not document contents or a storage backup.

          Verification

          A retrieved file's byte count and SHA-256 hash are compared with its receipt before download or preview. A previous successful check does not guarantee current availability.
